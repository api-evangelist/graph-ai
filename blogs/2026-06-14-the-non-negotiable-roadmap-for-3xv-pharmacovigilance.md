In the world of Patient Safety and vigilance, pharmacovigilance (PV), Cosmetovigilance, and Materiovigilance (collectively the “3xV”) , the introduction of new technology, even an AI-native solution, isn’t just a software or device install—it’s a transformation of a critical, highly-regulated process. While the AI gets the headlines, successful adoption always comes down to strong fundamentals of change management and onboarding.
